**Sentiment Classification Guidelines:**

**Negative Sentiment:** Customer exhibits clear signs of dissatisfaction including:
- Anger, frustration, or irritation in their voice
- Loud, aggressive, or hostile tone
- Complaints, criticism, or expressions of disappointment
- Impatience or annoyance with the service or resolution
- Threatening language or demands for escalation

**Important:** Negative sentiment should only be applied in cases of extreme and adverse conditions like verbal spat. You need to remain lenient while evaluating the sentiment, giving customers the benefit of the doubt when their emotional state is ambiguous.

**Neutral Sentiment:** Customer maintains an emotionally balanced interaction:
- Professional, matter-of-fact tone without strong emotional indicators
- Calm and composed demeanor throughout the conversation
- Neither particularly satisfied nor dissatisfied
- Focused on information exchange without emotional investment

**Positive Sentiment:** Customer demonstrates satisfaction and contentment:
- Warm, friendly, and appreciative tone
- Expressions of gratitude or satisfaction with the service
- Cooperative and pleasant interaction style
- Positive feedback or compliments about the resolution or agent
- Overall satisfied demeanor with the conversation outcome


**Role Definitions for Customer Profiling:**

**Agent:** A person representing the brand who plays a central role in providing service to the customer. Agents interact with chief technicians or technicians to arrange on-ground service and serve as the primary point of contact for customer inquiries and issue resolution.

**Customer:** A person who contacts the call center seeking to resolve an issue, make an inquiry, or receive service support for their account or connection.

**Technician:** A person representing the brand who provides on-ground service to resolve customer issues. Technicians are responsible for physical service delivery, installations, repairs, and technical support at customer locations.

**Chief Technician:** A senior technician who manages other technicians and coordinates between customers and agents. Chief technicians handle complex technical issues and oversee field service operations.

**Senior Agent:** A person representing the brand who provides escalated assistance when the current agent is unable to resolve the customer's issue. Calls are typically transferred to senior agents for complex cases requiring additional expertise or authority.

**Recorded Audio:** Any pre-recorded audio content in the call, including music, songs, caller tunes, ring tones, background music, voice mail messages, or automated announcements.

**Additional Participants:** When additional participants join the call, they should be identified as senior agent, technician, chief technician, or other call center personnel based on their role and context in the conversation.
